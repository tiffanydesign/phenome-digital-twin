Chronological age 41 is the fixed centre of the arc. The solid arc and the marker run from that centre to the reading, in the same colour as the number.

06

Status pill

Four states share one ladder, least to most severe. Tint at 12% of the status hue, text at the -ink variant, so the pill states the interpretation before the number does. Action is not a fifth state on that ladder, it's a suggested next step, so it gets its own neutral shape and sits underneath the reading, never inside the row.

Normal

Inside range. No action.

Noteworthy

Just outside range. Worth a note, not a recheck.

Monitor

Borderline. Recheck next scan.

Alert

High signal. Clinician review.

ApoB 1.06g/L Alert
Recheck on a fasting sample

Action reuses the same 12%-tint logic in reverse, an outline instead of a fill, ink-600 instead of a status hue, so it can never be mistaken for a fifth severity colour.

Wording

Each state keeps one word across the whole product. Monitor in the pill, Monitor in the filter, Monitor in the export. Action follows the same rule, but stays a recommendation, never a status.

07

Projection with benchmark

Where a measure has a trajectory, the block leads with the three numbers it exists to deliver, then shows the shape behind them. Solid is measured, dashed is projected, and the plot carries nothing else: a limit belongs in the pill and the note, not as another line across the same space. The cohort median stays neutral, it's context, not a verdict.

08

Panel grid

Group by clinical panel, four columns, name left and value right. Every value sits on a tinted ground in its own status hue, so the panel is read by colour before it is read by number.

09

Cohort matrix

For population views: cohorts down, measures across, cells tinted by the size of the effect. Same single hue as the heatmap, opacity carrying the magnitude, not a second colour carrying direction. A cell with nothing worth reporting shows a dot.

10

Provenance mark

CC BY names four things: the work, its author, the licence, and that the work was changed. How many works are on screen decides the shape. One work has nothing to factor out, so the credit is a single line. A field full of them shares the licence and the modification, so those lead once and the list varies only work and author. One ring per field either way, and never a fill — a filled shape here is a status.

Hover is not the record

A credit that lives only in :hover is absent from touch, from print and from an export, and an absent credit is a breached licence. The panel is markup at all times; the ring decides only what is on screen, and on paper it prints as the field's footer.

Which form

Count the borrowed works in the field, not the organs on the rail. The home page shows one model and takes the line; the body page carries the base mesh plus every organ and takes the list.

06 · Glass surfaces

Real glass has a cut edge, not a smudge, a thin, almost unblurred line where its top face catches light, and no shadow at all beneath it, so it never reads as a separate layer floating above the page. A second, quieter recipe covers the larger panels that hold cards like these rather than floating on their own.

Fill

White at roughly 18% opacity for a card's cut face. A panel drops to about 9%, it's meant to hold its own weight across a much larger area, never to compete with what sits inside it.

Edge

No border. inset 0 1px 1px white, almost no blur, draws the top face of a card's cut; a 1px ring closes the rest of it so the edge reads as continuous, not just a bright top.

Refraction

backdrop-filter: blur(38px) saturate(190%) on a card, blur(56px) on a panel. Pushed until the scene behind the glass is unmistakably there, not just implied.

Panel

A panel isn't a bigger card. It trades the crisp cut edge for one soft, wide catch of light, because a sheet this size reads as the room's own glass wall, not something held up to a source.

Scale

The track fill reuses the same status colours as the rest of the system, brand blue for a normal reading, amber for one worth a second look, nothing invented just for this section.
