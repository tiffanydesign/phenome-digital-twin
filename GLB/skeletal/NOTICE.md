# Lumbar and hip skeletal model

`lumbar-hip.glb` is a browser-ready extract containing the bilateral femurs
and hip bones, sacrum, L1-L5 vertebrae, and L1-S1 intervertebral discs from the
Anatria3D male skeletal atlas.
