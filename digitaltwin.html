02 · Type scale

Every role, largest to smallest, drawn at its true size. Thirteen rungs exist; a report page reaches for six of them, plus one more for markers, and never invents a fourteenth.

The report ladder · six rungs for content, one for markers
80--text-displayA hero numeral, in the mono. One per page at most.
44--text-title-lgThe page title, once, in Contralto Medium.
33--kpi-value-lgA measured value, or a finding stated as a word — so the number pinned on the organ and the same number in the rail are one size, and the eye pairs them.
24--kpi-value-smA card's title, in Contralto Xsmall.
18--text-labelNames and supporting prose: everything that says what a value is.
16--text-dataMeta: glosses, captions, scale ticks, row descriptions.
14--text-microMarkers only — the status pill and the action tag, one rung under the meta they sit beside. Not a rung for prose.

Rank inside a rung is carried by ink, weight and tracking, never by a seventh size. Three inks and no more: ink-900 for what you are meant to read, ink-label for supporting prose and units, ink-600 for meta. ink-400 is not a text ink at these sizes — it measures about 2.9:1 on the page's own ground.

02

Pinned readout

Values sit in the margin and reach back to the body along a hairline, so the reader sees where a measurement was taken before reading it. The leader is 1.5px at 45% navy.

03

Zoned range track

A single value on a named scale, with the clinical boundaries printed where they fall.

04

Segmented dot meter

A count of discrete units against a target. Colour and a boundary marker carry the status before the number does.

Filtration rate eGFR · 90 to 120 expected
05

Arc index dial

For a derived age or index where the question is which side of centre the reading falls. The arc has a true midpoint and both ends are named, so the reading is directional.

Biological age
37 years 4 yrs younger

Chronological age 41 is the fixed centre of the arc. The solid arc and the marker run from that centre to the reading, in the same colour as the number.

07

Projection with benchmark

Where a measure has a trajectory, the block leads with the three numbers it exists to deliver, then shows the shape behind them. Solid is measured, dashed is projected, and the plot carries nothing else: a limit belongs in the pill and the note, not as another line across the same space. The cohort median stays neutral, it's context, not a verdict.

08

Panel grid

Group by clinical panel, four columns, name left and value right. Every value sits on a tinted ground in its own status hue, so the panel is read by colour before it is read by number.

09

Cohort matrix

For population views: cohorts down, measures across, cells tinted by the size of the effect. Same single hue as the heatmap, opacity carrying the magnitude, not a second colour carrying direction. A cell with nothing worth reporting shows a dot.

10

Marking gallery

Square tiles on a fixed grid, the ordinal in the corner, the detected region ringed inside the crop so the eye compares shapes. Selection is shown as a border on the tile.
